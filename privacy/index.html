AffiliateOS - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Overview

AffiliateOS is a privately operated application for managing affiliate products and preparing social media content. This policy explains the intended handling of information within the application.

2. Information Processed

AffiliateOS may process product information, uploaded media, content metadata, and information from social media accounts connected with user authorization.

3. TikTok Integration

When TikTok integration is enabled and a user authorizes access, AffiliateOS may access basic account information and request permission to upload video drafts. The requested access will depend on the authorized scopes.

4. Data Storage

AffiliateOS currently uses a locally hosted application environment. Connected platform credentials and account information, when supported, should be protected against unauthorized access.

5. Data Sharing

Information may be transmitted to connected platforms when the user initiates an authorized operation. AffiliateOS does not offer user information for sale to advertisers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is managed within the application's storage environment. Data deletion and disconnection procedures will be documented as platform integrations are implemented.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to AffiliateOS features, data processing, or third-party integrations.
